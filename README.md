# DealFlow360 Team 57

## An Intelligent, Self-Governing Sales Operations Platform

DealFlow360 is a B2B sales operations platform that manages the complete
quotation-to-cash journey:

**Quotation → Discount Evaluation → Risk Assessment → Automatic Approval
→ Upsell/Cross-sell → Warehouse Fulfillment → Hybrid Billing → Customer
Negotiation → Payment → Deal Health**

The platform is designed around configurable business rules rather than
hard-coded demo logic.

---

## 1. Problem Statement

Traditional sales systems often treat quotations as static documents and
require users to manually coordinate discount approvals, inventory
availability, customer negotiations, and billing.

DealFlow360 provides a connected workflow where:

- Sales representatives create and revise quotations.
- Discount limits are evaluated against customer tiers and product
  categories.
- Risky quotations are automatically routed for approval.
- Managers and Finance can approve/reject according to configured
  approval chains.
- Upsell and cross-sell recommendations can be shown while creating a
  quotation.
- Inventory can be fulfilled from multiple warehouses.
- Backorders can be recorded when stock is insufficient.
- One-time and recurring products can exist in the same commercial
  order while receiving separate billing treatment.
- Customers can negotiate quotations through a restricted portal.
- Negotiated terms are re-evaluated and can automatically return to
  approval.
- Payments update invoice status.
- Deal-health alerts identify stalled, anomalous, or slipping deals.
